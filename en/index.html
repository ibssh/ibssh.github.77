Celebrity Project

Project: An integrated turnkey project covering planning, design, construction, engineering management, and delivery for 500 townhouses in Pioneer Town, San Bernardino County, California.

Parcel No.: 20215

Project No.: 21-21S

Award Date: April 18, 2022

Office of the U.S. Courts, 211 Golden Gate Avenue, San Francisco, California 94118-4397

Construction Contract Award Letter

To: IBS Construction Decoration Ltd.,

Address: Zone B, Room 1489, 1/F, Building 12, No. 1188 Huyi Road, Jiading District, Shanghai, China

Awarding Authority: Chief Justice of the Superior Court of California

James C Duff, Director of the Administrative office of the United States courts would like to inform you that the Government of California in collaboration with the Administrative office of the United States courts is willing to enter into an agreement with IBS Construction Decoration Ltd., for the award of the contract for the "preconstruction and construction of 500 townhouses in Pioneer Town " with the "Project No.: 21-21S".

The project held its groundbreaking ceremony in California in June 2025 and is currently in the planning adjustment and construction phase.
